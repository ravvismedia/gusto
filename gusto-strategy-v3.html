GustoTrsral carries 34,782 followers and international recognition built over years, first as a respected manager of musicians and now as an artist stepping forward in his own right. The account has posted only twice in the past month, and average engagement sits close to zero. Reach tells a different story: the two posts that did go out still reached 1,238 people organically, with an average reach of 619 per post. The audience has not disappeared. It has simply not been spoken to. Inside Ghana, where GustoTrsral is now based and building from, he remains largely unknown. Outside Ghana, recognition already exists.

O

Objectives

What six months delivers
  • Posting cadence moves from 2 posts a month to 4 to 5 posts a week starting Month 1, the foundation every other number below depends on.
  • Instagram grows from 34,782 to 35,022 followers, a modest but realistic increase, with meaningful growth specifically from Ghana.
  • Average engagement rate rises from close to 0% to 8% by Month 6.
  • Organic reach grows from 1,238 a month to a combined 3,000 a month across all 3 platforms.
  • TikTok reaches 240 followers and YouTube reaches 150 subscribers, both from a standing start.
  • The first TRILAND edition reaches 10,000 combined streams within 60 days of release.
  • GustoTrsral and TRILAND reach the first page of Google search results, top 10, by Month 6.
