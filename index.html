Genre He Created

GustoTrsral carries an audience of 34.8K followers and international recognition built over years, first as a respected manager of musicians and now as an artist stepping forward in his own right. That recognition has traveled well outside Ghana. Inside Ghana, where he is now based and building from, he remains largely unknown. A recent return to posting has reopened the account after a long period of silence. The opportunity is direct: an audience already exists, a story already carries weight, and Ghana has not yet met either.

O

Objectives

What six months delivers
  • Ghana knows GustoTrsral by name, story, and sound, not as the manager he once was.
  • The existing 34.8K following is active and growing, converted from dormant to engaged.
  • TRILAND is established as a genre with a real audience behind it, through one properly sequenced release.
  • Music as a business becomes the signature that separates GustoTrsral from every emerging artist around him.
  • A brand strong enough to carry the label and C.H.A.L.E Global into their next phase.

Strategy

Four pillars
The Reintroduction

Ghana meets the artist behind the name, told as a homecoming, not a launch from zero.

The Reactivation

34.8K followers already exist. The work is turning that number into an engaged, local, and pan African audience.

Organic Before Paid

Advertising spend follows proof. Every dollar amplifies what is already working.

One Identity, Every Platform

The same voice, sound, and story, everywhere GustoTrsral appears.

The approach draws on patterns behind Burna Boy's campaign execution, Future's consistency, Central Cee's short form reach, Akon's home first model, and Pitbull's brand equity, distilled into a single system built for GustoTrsral and TRILAND.

Tactics

The plan, month by month
01

Foundation

Rebuild Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube to one consistent standard. Lock the visual identity and the voice. No advertising spend.

Introduce Ghana to the man behind the 34.8K, through the Haiti, Nashville, and Ghana journey. First activation campaign. Baseline metrics set.

03

Roll out the first TRILAND edition as a fully sequenced campaign. Targeted awareness advertising begins here, behind content already proven organically.

04

The Brand

Reinforce music as a business as GustoTrsral's signature positioning against every other emerging artist in the space.

05

Global Reach

Connect Ghana, Haiti, and Nashville into one coordinated push. Conversion advertising limited to proven content.

Full performance review against every objective above, and the foundation set for what comes next.
